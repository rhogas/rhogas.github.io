  We investigate whether AI-based PINN and DeepONet surrogate models can learn simplified ADCIRC-like shallow-water dynamics from a small synthetic ensemble with varying forcing and bathymetry. We compare both approaches in terms of predictive accuracy, generalization, computational cost, and physical consistency, providing a preliminary framework for more complex ADCIRC-type coastal surrogate modeling.
